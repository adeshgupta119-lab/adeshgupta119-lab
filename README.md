### 🏛️ Enterprise Architecture & Technical Competencies

<details open>
<summary><b>1. Tenant & Management Group Hierarchy (CAF Architecture)</b></summary>
<br>

- **Hierarchy Blueprint:** Architected Root Tenant Hierarchy (`platform-mg`, `sandbox-mg`, `decommissioned-mg`) isolating platform services from application workloads.
- **Subscription Boundaries:** Designed dedicated subscriptions across lifecycle stages (Identity, Connectivity, Management, Dev, UAT, Prod).
- **Governance & Policy:** Enterprise-wide Azure Policy & Initiatives assignments, automated compliance auditing, RBAC group mappings, and documented policy exemption lifecycle.
- **Microsoft Entra ID:** Conditional Access, PIM (Privileged Identity Management), and enterprise directory synchronization.
</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>2. Enterprise Governance & Identity (Cloud Adoption Framework)</b></summary>
<br>

- **CAF Lifecycle Alignment:** Covering **Strategy ➔ Plan ➔ Ready ➔ Adopt ➔ Govern ➔ Secure & Manage**.
- **Centralized Inspection & Egress:** Single-pane traffic filtering, next-gen firewall inspection, and strictly controlled egress routing.
- **Security Center & Defender:** Continuous posture monitoring using Microsoft Defender for Cloud and Secure Score improvement.
</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>3. Hub & Spoke Networking & Zero-Trust Security</b></summary>
<br>

- **Network Design:** HUB VNet peering with Spoke VNets; On-premises CIDR assessment & enterprise IPAM.
- **Traffic Routing:** User Defined Routes (UDR) forcing all outbound/inter-spoke traffic through centralized Firewalls.
- **Private Access:** Zero public endpoints via Azure Private Endpoints, Private DNS Zones, Azure Bastion, VPN Gateway, and ExpressRoute circuits.
- **Multi-Level Security:** Defenses across **Identity Level**, **Networking Level**, **Governance Level**, and **Compute Level**.
</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>4. Terraform Landing Zone Automation (IaC)</b></summary>
<br>

- **Modular Infrastructure:** Highly reusable, version-controlled Terraform modules adhering to HashiCorp best practices.
- **State Security:** Remote State stored in encrypted storage accounts with strict Least Privilege RBAC access and encryption-at-rest.
- **Quality Gates:** Integrated `tflint`, secret scanning (`gitleaks`), automated cost breakdown (`infracost`), manual staging approvals, and automated **Pipeline Drift Detection**.
</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>5. Azure DevSecOps Pipelines & Branching Governance</b></summary>
<br>

- **Branching Strategy:** Trunk-based development, short-lived feature branches, mandatory pull requests (PR), and CODEOWNERS approvals.
- **Branch Protection:** Strict branch policies prohibiting direct commits or deletion of the `main` branch.
- **Pipeline Templates:** Standardized YAML pipeline templates for **Validate & Test ➔ Build ➔ Deployment ➔ Terraform Apply ➔ Automated Rollback**.
- **Automation & Middleware:** Parallel job execution, custom data scripts for OS bootstrap, and secure secrets injection via Azure Key Vault.
</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>6. Cloud Migration Lifecycle & Execution (100+ Workloads)</b></summary>
<br>

- **Pre-Migration:** Discovery & assessment, application dependency mapping, readiness matrices, pre-migration security checklists.
- **Cutover Execution:** Coordinated cutover windows, data synchronization, DNS redirection, and rollback contingency plans.
- **Hypercare & Post-Migration:** 24/7 post-cutover hypercare monitoring, SLA stabilization, and documentation handoff.
</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>7. FinOps & Cost Engineering</b></summary>
<br>

- **Waste Elimination:** Automated identification of unattached managed disks, idle IP addresses, oversized compute instances, and unneeded log ingestions.
- **Cost Reduction:** Automated schedule for non-production VM shutdown/startup, Reserved Instances (RI) / Savings Plans forecasting, and optimized backup retention policies.
</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>8. BCDR & Observability</b></summary>
<br>

- **Disaster Recovery (BCDR):** Architected resilient storage redundancy (LRS, ZRS, GRS) and VM Scale Sets (VMSS) across primary and secondary Azure paired regions.
- **RTO / RPO Assurance:** Tested failover blueprints designed around aggressive enterprise RTO and RPO targets.
- **Monitoring:** End-to-end telemetry via Azure Monitor, Log Analytics Workspaces, and custom alerting thresholds.
</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>9. Vendor & Delivery Leadership</b></summary>
<br>

- **Vendor Governance:** SOW formulation, deliverables tracking, sprint planning, risk registers, and milestone sign-offs.
- **Evaluation & Contracting:** RFP/RFI lifecycle, technical scoring matrices, architectural capability reviews, and commercial negotiations.
- **Team Enablement:** Conducting senior technical interviews, RACI accountability frameworks, and Knowledge Transfer (KT) sessions.
</details>
